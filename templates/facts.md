# Facts

Every number, name, quote and claim that may appear on screen. No row, no screen.

| Item | Exact value as shown | Source (link or file) | Checked on | Label on screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| {what it is} | {the value exactly as rendered} | {where it comes from} | {YYYY-MM-DD} | {"Sample data", "Concept study" or none} |

Derived figures (totals, a balance after a set-aside) name the rows they come from
and must add up. Never round up, add "+", or turn a target into a result. A figure
that only appears in passing, such as a frame inside a transition, still needs a row.
